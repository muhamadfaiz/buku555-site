Buku 555 — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 November 2026

The short version: Buku 555 stores your expenses on your own iPhone. There is no account, no login, and no server of ours. We cannot see your spending, and we do not collect analytics, run ads, or track you. The only time data leaves your phone for reading a receipt is when you turn on the optional Google AI feature and use your own key.

Who we are

Buku 555 is a personal expense-tracking app for iPhone. This policy explains how the app handles your information. It applies to the app and to this page. If you have questions, contact us using the address at the bottom of this page.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you enter is saved only on your iPhone, in the app’s own on-device database. This includes:

  • Expenses you add or confirm: shop name, amount, date, your notes, tags, and line items.
  • Receipt photos you scan, kept with the matching expense on your device.
  • Settings you choose, such as your monthly budget, tag names and colours, shop rules, currency, and your notification and receipt-reading preferences.
  • Draft transactions created from Wallet card taps, which wait in Review until you confirm or discard them.

This data is stored locally and is not sent to us. We run no server and have no account system, so there is nothing for us to read and we never receive your data. It lives only on your device, and is included only in your own iPhone and iCloud device backups, which Apple manages and you control — not in any backup of ours. Your information otherwise travels off the device only if you take it there, using the export and backup feature or the optional Google AI setting described below.

No accounts, no login

You do not create an account and you never sign in. The app works fully offline. Because there is no account, we hold none of your personal data on our side.

How card taps work

When you set it up, a Shortcuts personal automation on your iPhone hands a Wallet card transaction to the app through the “Log card tap” action. The merchant, amount, card name and time are passed straight from your phone to the app on the same phone. This happens locally on your device. It does not go through us or any server, and it does not read your card itself — it uses the transaction that Apple Pay already recorded and that the Shortcuts automation is allowed to see.

Receipt scanning

By default, receipts are read on your iPhone using Apple’s on-device camera and text recognition. The photo and the recognised text stay on your device. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else for the default scanning to work.

Google AI receipt reading (optional)

Google AI is not on by default. It is used only if you both enter your own Google AI key in Settings and choose Google AI as the reader.

  • When it is on, each receipt photo you scan is sent from your iPhone directly to Google to be read, and the recognised details are sent back to your phone.
  • The key is yours and is stored securely in your iPhone’s Keychain. It is not sent to us, and we do not have a copy.
  • Google’s use of anything you send is covered by Google’s own privacy terms, not ours. If you do not want your receipt photos sent to Google, leave the reader on “On this iPhone” or turn it off.
  • If there is no key, or Google cannot be reached, the app reads the receipt on your phone instead.

No analytics, no ads, no tracking

  • No analytics or usage tracking. The app has no built-in analytics or telemetry and does not report how you use it.
  • No advertising. There are no ads and no ad networks.
  • No third-party trackers. The app embeds no advertising or tracking SDKs, does not use the device’s advertising identifier, and does not link your data to other apps or websites. It does not use this page or the app to set tracking cookies.

Notifications

With your permission, the app posts local notifications to tell you a new card-tap draft is waiting to review. These are generated on your phone by the app itself. They are not push notifications from a server, and they contain no data sent to us.

Export and backup (you are in control)

You can export your expenses as a CSV file or save a backup file using the standard iOS share sheet. Where that file goes next — Files, a notes app, email, cloud storage — is entirely your choice. We do not receive it. You can delete any expense in the app at any time, and deleting or uninstalling the app removes its on-device data from the phone.

Camera permission

The app asks for camera access so you can scan receipts. It uses the camera only while you are actively scanning. Receipt photos are saved on your device with the expense and are never uploaded to us.

Children

Buku 555 is a personal finance tool for adults and is not directed to children. It collects no personal data of any user, and it has no social, chat or user-generated content features.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s privacy behaviour changes, we will update this page and revise the effective date above. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
